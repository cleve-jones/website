About Cleve

Keep pushing.
Keep fighting.
Keep organizing.

That’s what Cleve Jones learned from his mentors, Harvey Milk and Art Agnos. He co-founded the San Francisco AIDS Foundation, founded the AIDS Memorial Quilt and spent two decades organizing for working families. Now he’s sharing those lessons with young leaders.
